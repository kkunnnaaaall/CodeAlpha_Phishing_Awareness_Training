# Phishing Awareness Training

A modern cybersecurity awareness presentation focused on phishing detection, prevention, and defense strategies in 2026.  
This project explains modern phishing techniques, social engineering tactics, and best practices for protecting organizations against cyber threats.

---

## Overview

Phishing remains one of the leading causes of corporate data breaches worldwide.  
This training presentation covers:

- Modern phishing attacks
- Social engineering techniques
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Fake website detection
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Real-world phishing scenarios
- Cybersecurity best practices

---

## Topics Covered

### 1. The Scale of the Threat
- AI-generated phishing attacks
- Billions of phishing emails sent daily
- Personalized attack campaigns

### 2. Core Concepts
- Standard Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Social Engineering

### 3. Modern Attack Vectors
- Email phishing
- QR-code phishing (Quishing)
- SMS phishing (Smishing)
- Voice phishing (Vishing)

### 4. Spotting Red Flags
- Fake urgency
- Suspicious email domains
- Unknown attachments
- Generic greetings

### 5. Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Executive impersonation
- Fake invoice scams
- Financial fraud tactics

### 6. Fake Website Detection
- Domain mismatch detection
- Hover-before-click technique
- HTTPS misconceptions

### 7. Defense Strategies
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Out-of-band verification
- Reporting phishing attempts

### 8. Interactive Scenarios
- Fake IT support email example
- CEO impersonation SMS attack example

## Learning Objectives

After completing this training, users will be able to:

- Identify phishing emails and fake websites
- Recognize social engineering tactics
- Avoid credential theft attacks
- Detect Business Email Compromise scams
- Apply cybersecurity best practices

---

## Key Takeaways

- Never trust urgent financial requests without verification
- Always verify suspicious links before clicking
- Enable MFA wherever possible
- Report phishing attempts immediately
- Cybersecurity awareness is everyone's responsibility
